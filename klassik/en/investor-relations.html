Investment story

Why 1daily. In five points.

01

The market

Gold is one of the most liquid markets in the world, with around USD 361bn traded every day (World Gold Council, 2025). Entries and exits are possible at any time, and our capital does not move the price. Switzerland is also the centre of the physical gold trade.

02

The competitive edge

A system rather than individual trades: our own analysis software (Goldcheck), a technically enforced risk system (RiskGuard by 1daily) and price data going back to 2003. Every trading day adds new data and improves the system. The software is the intellectual property of 1daily AG.

03

The revenue model

Today: income from proprietary trading. In progress: selling RiskGuard by 1daily as software. Planned: Goldcheck as a subscription and a copy trading offering. Three clearly separate paths that reinforce each other.

04

The founder

Over more than 25 years, Ivan Schmid has built several companies: a web CMS with more than 50,000 users (Comvation), the first half-price pass for hotels (Hotelcard) and a regulated payment institution for Swiss SMEs (Payrexx). He knows how to lead teams of more than 50 people.

05

Capital with a clear purpose

The CHF 2.0m seed round funds the expansion of proprietary trading (50%), product development (25%), growth (15%) and operations and reserves (10%). The next steps are defined as milestones.
