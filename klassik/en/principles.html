Principles

How we work. And what we stand for.

Seven principles guide every decision at 1daily AG: in trading, in developing our software and in how we deal with our shareholders.

Our approach

Three roles. Clearly separated.

We do not predict the market. We wait, with discipline, for confirmation of the forces that actually move it. To do so, we split the work into three roles that keep each other in check.

AI analyses

Goldcheck evaluates price data, macro data, institutional market data and news across six layers and delivers a reasoned signal with a confidence score.

People trade

The trader checks the five conditions of the 1daily Method, sets entry, stop-loss and target, makes the decision and carries the responsibility.

The system protects

RiskGuard by 1daily sits between trader and broker and only lets orders through that comply with the risk rules.

01

Systematic, not speculativeThe 1daily Method

We trade gold only at institutional price zones, only after a proven liquidity sweep, only on a confirmed change of direction and only with entry, stop-loss and target defined in advance. If one condition is missing, there is no trade.

  • Bank level: institutional price zone
  • Incomplete candle close: the candle closes back inside the range
  • The candle leaving the level is larger than the ATR
  • Momentum: volume and delta in the new direction
  • SBU entry with a limit order in the unmitigated zone
To the 1daily Method →
02

Capital protection firstRisk management

Protecting capital comes before any single market opinion. Our risk rules are not negotiable and are enforced technically, not by a person’s form on the day.

  • At most 3% loss per day, then trading stops for the day
  • At most three positions at the same time
  • Stop-loss from entry, never moved or widened
  • No larger positions after losses, no hedge that merely postpones a loss
  • No new positions from 30 minutes before to 15 minutes after major economic data
03

Technology serving peopleGoldcheck and RiskGuard by 1daily

Artificial intelligence does not replace judgement, it sharpens it. Goldcheck shows not only whether the market is bullish or bearish but also why, and actively outputs “No Trade” when uncertainty is high.

A signal alone is never a reason to trade. What counts are the five conditions of the method, checked and decided by a person.

04

Process over outcomeDiscipline

No single trade decides success. We measure ourselves by the quality of every decision. A loss is a planned operating expense; a rule violation is not.

  • Every trade is recorded in the journal
  • Rule violations and their costs are made visible
  • No trading when tired, frustrated or stressed
05

TransparencyClarity as a form of respect

We say what we know and what we do not. The results of our two live accounts are verified by Myfxbook, including the first months, when they were still traded without Goldcheck and RiskGuard by 1daily.

We promise no returns and name risks openly. Investors receive the full business plan and track record.
