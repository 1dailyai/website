1daily AG · Swiss stock corporation · Steffisburg, Switzerland
Swiss trading technology for the gold market

Artificial intelligence analyses. People trade. The system protects.

1daily AG is a Swiss technology company. We analyse the gold market with our own software, trade by a fixed rulebook and protect every risk technically.

Our principlesFor investors
What sets us apart

A clear system. Applied consistently.

We do not predict the market. We wait, with discipline, for confirmation of the forces that actually move it, and we limit the risk before a position is opened.

Systematic · Disciplined · Transparent · Swiss

01

Systematic

We trade by the 1daily Method: five conditions in a fixed order. If one is missing, there is no trade.

02

Capital protection first

At most 3% risk per day, at most three positions, a stop-loss from entry. RiskGuard by 1daily enforces these rules technically.

03

AI-powered analysis

Goldcheck condenses technicals, macro data, institutional market data and news across six layers into a reasoned signal. A person makes the decision.

Focused
