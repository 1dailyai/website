1daily AG · Steffisburg, Switzerland
Vision

Better decisions. Every day.

We combine data, artificial intelligence and human experience so that decisions in the financial markets become clearer. First in our own trading, then for our customers.

Vision

A Swiss company that uses its own technology to create value in the gold market, systematically and for the long term.

Mission

We analyse the market with artificial intelligence, trade by fixed rules and protect every risk technically. What works in our own trading, we make available as software.

Our principles

Three rules. No exceptions.

01
Process over outcome

We measure ourselves by the quality of every decision, not by individual trades.

02
Risk first

Every position has a fixed, limited risk before it is opened.

03
Clarity over complexity

Every signal is reasoned and traceable.

The founder

“The summit was the result. Endurance was the achievement.”

In 2011, on an expedition with Ueli Steck, Ivan Schmid waited ten days at 6,300 metres because weather and wind ruled out any summit push. The same patience and consistency shape 1daily AG today: clear rules, clean execution, step by step.

Ivan Schmid · Founder, 1daily AG
