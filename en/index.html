1daily AG · Steffisburg, Switzerland
1daily AG · Swiss trading technology

Artificial intelligence analyses.
People trade.
The system protects.

1daily AG is a Swiss technology company for the gold market. Our software Goldcheck analyses the market with artificial intelligence and delivers precise signals. Experienced traders turn them into short, targeted trades, and RiskGuard by 1daily protects every position automatically. Our goal: to grow our shareholders’ capital sustainably over the long term.

What sets us apart

We do not predict the market. We wait, with discipline, for its confirmation.

Systematic · Disciplined · Transparent · Swiss

Our principles
01

Systematic

We trade by the 1daily Method: five conditions in a fixed order. If one is missing, there is no trade.

02

Capital protection first

At most 3% risk per day, at most three positions, a stop-loss from entry. RiskGuard by 1daily enforces these rules technically.

03

AI-powered analysis

Goldcheck condenses technicals, macro data, institutional market data and news across six layers into a reasoned signal. A person makes the decision.

Focused
